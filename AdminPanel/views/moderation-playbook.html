Moderation Playbook
Scenario Response Guide

Use this workspace for consistent decisions, safer escalation, and reliable case documentation across your team.

🧭 Moderation Decision Framework

Apply the same sequence for every case: verify context, contain immediate harm, decide proportionate action, then document clearly.

1) Verify Context

Check full message history, timestamps, previous warnings, and whether this is a repeated pattern.

2) Contain Harm

Stop active disruption first: remove harmful content, mute/timeout if needed, and lock impacted channels.

3) Decide Action

Use proportional enforcement: warning for first low-risk violations, stronger actions for severe or repeated abuse.

4) Record & Communicate

Log actor, target, evidence, rule violated, and action rationale so appeals can be reviewed fairly.

🚨 Spam / Raid Scenario
Severity: High
Objective: Stop active disruption quickly and restore normal operations with minimal collateral damage.

First 2 Minutes

Contain spread first, then recover visibility.

  • Lock high-traffic channels or apply aggressive slowmode.
  • Mass-timeout obvious spam accounts and pause invites if required.
  • Assign one mod to enforcement and one mod to evidence logging.

Immediate Actions (0-2 min)

  • Enable slowmode or temporarily lock affected channels.
  • Timeout or remove obvious raid/spam accounts in waves.
  • Disable invite links if attack appears coordinated.

Stabilization (2-10 min)

  • Review audit log for mass joins and role abuse signals.
  • Tune automod thresholds for links, mentions, and repeated text.
  • Post a short status update for members.
🧱 Harassment / Bullying Scenario
Severity: High
Objective: Protect the targeted user, stop repeated abuse, and maintain a documented behavior trail.

First 2 Minutes

Protect targeted users and preserve evidence before cleanup.

  • Capture relevant messages and IDs before deletion or edits.
  • Separate aggressor from target with timeout or channel restriction.
  • Notify another moderator for second-review if severity is unclear.

Evidence & Safety

  • Capture messages, attachments, and timestamps before deletion.
  • Separate involved users and remove public escalation.
  • Check prior warnings and related incidents.

Enforcement

  • Issue warning for first lower-severity offense.
  • Use timeout for repeated targeted behavior.
  • Escalate to ban for severe or persistent harassment.
⛔ Hate Speech / Extremism Scenario
Severity: Critical
Objective: Remove harmful content immediately and enforce a strict, policy-aligned response.

First 2 Minutes

Immediate removal and strict enforcement are the priority.

  • Delete visible harmful content and snapshot evidence internally.
  • Apply policy-level action without delay for severe hate content.
  • Escalate to senior staff if there are threat indicators.

Critical Response

  • Delete hateful content and preserve evidence for case records.
  • Apply immediate severe action according to policy baseline.
  • Escalate to admin/owner if content implies threats.

Post-Action Follow-up

  • Document exact rule violated and rationale for action.
  • Review related channels for coordinated behavior.
  • Reinforce community standards if incident was visible.
🆘 Self-Harm Risk Scenario
Severity: Critical
Objective: Prioritize user safety, reduce harm exposure, and escalate urgently using your server policy.

First 2 Minutes

Treat this as urgent safety handling with calm escalation.

  • Assign a senior moderator to direct the response immediately.
  • Move interaction to a private support context where possible.
  • Share approved support resources and start internal handoff notes.

Safety First

  • Move discussion to private, supportive communication where possible.
  • Share approved crisis resources and encourage reaching trusted help.
  • Escalate internally to senior staff immediately.

Moderation Boundaries

  • Do not dismiss or challenge distress statements.
  • Avoid public debate around the incident.
  • Log actions and handoff notes for continuity.
⚖️ Appeals Handling Scenario
Severity: Medium
Objective: Review cases neutrally, evaluate new evidence, and make consistent final decisions.

First 2 Minutes

Set review scope, then validate evidence quality.

  • Open original case log and confirm rule references.
  • Separate new evidence from unsupported claims.
  • Assign independent reviewer if the original moderator is involved.

Review Workflow

  • Verify original evidence and rule mapping.
  • Check whether punishment level matched violation severity.
  • Prioritize new verifiable evidence provided in appeal.

Decision Outcomes

  • Uphold action when evidence and policy alignment are clear.
  • Reduce action where severity was overestimated.
  • Revoke action only when the case is materially unsound.
