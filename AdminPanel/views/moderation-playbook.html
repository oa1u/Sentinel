Moderation Philosophy
The Core 4-Step Engagement Process
Universal
Step 01
Understand First
Take a breath and check the context. Look at what happened right before the incident, account ages, and history. There's almost always a bigger picture.
Step 02
Stop the Bleeding
If things are getting out of hand, hit pause. Use a quick timeout or delete messages to stop active harm before it escalates.
Step 03
Be Fair & Consistent
Guide your actions using our Matrix below. Treat everyone on the server the exact same way, regardless of who they are.
Step 04
Leave a Trail
Write down why you took action like someone else has to read it tomorrow. Clear logs make appeals so much easier to handle.
Escalation Matrix
Standard penalties for common offense tiers
Minor Offense (spam, rude, caps) Warn / 1h Timeout
Moderate Offense (harassment, slurs, advertising) 24h Timeout / Kick
Severe Offense (raid, nsfw, threats) Ban
Critical Offense (doxxing, illegal content) Perm Ban + Report
Mass Mention Spam / Raids
Detected via AutoMod or sudden influx of pings
Critical

If you suddenly see multiple users or generic bot accounts dropping a ton of @everyone pings or repeating the same message holding links, you're likely looking at a raid. Don't panic, just lock it down.

Appeal Review Process
How to assess unban requests
Procedure
Check 1
Cool Down Period
Have they been away long enough to think about it? Generally, we look for at least 30 days unless there's hard proof we made a mistake up front.
Check 2
Taking Responsibility
Do they own up to what they did? If our logs show they broke a rule but their appeal says "I didn't do anything," that's usually a quick denial.
Check 3
Attitude Matters
Are they respectfully asking for a second chance? If an appeal comes in full of anger or insults, close it immediately.
