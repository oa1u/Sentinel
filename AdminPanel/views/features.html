Keep your community safe with enforcement tools, case tracking, and audit visibility.

Security & Admin Panel

Account protection, audit visibility, and admin-grade tooling built into the panel.
🧭 Security center: session tracking, login events, geo insights, and risk snapshots
🖼️ Profile workspace: upload your panel avatar, keep your profile looking consistent, and manage account details in one place
🔐 2FA support: TOTP enrollment, verification, and recovery-ready flows
🛡️ CSRF protection: hardened mutating requests with auto-refresh tokens
🔗 Discord linking: connect your Discord account, run verification checks, and fall back to your Discord avatar inside the panel when needed
📡 Internal webhooks: site-to-bot events for audits and notifications
📨 Account recovery: password reset flows, email delivery logging, and security notifications
🧾 Recovery codes: fallback account access with dedicated recovery workflows when normal login is unavailable
🗄️ Operational safeguards: database backups, session controls, CAPTCHA protection, and admin tools that help keep the panel stable
💾 Server backup center: inspect backups before restoring, review risks ahead of time, follow restore progress live, and pick back up after a refresh
