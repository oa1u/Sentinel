Privacy Policy

Transparent overview of how we handle, store, and protect your data.

📌 The Basics

Hi! This Privacy Policy explains how ("we", "us") handles your information. We built this bot to help you manage your community, not to spy on you.

By using the bot or this dashboard, you're cool with how we handle things described here.

Heads Up: Every Discord server is different. The server owners who use our bot might have their own rules. We provide the tools, but they decide how to use them (like what words to ban).

📩 Got Questions? Ask in the GitHub Repo.

📦 What We Collect
  • Your Profile: We save your Discord ID, username, and avatar so we know who's who.
  • Server Settings: We store your server's config like channel IDs, role setups, and welcome messages so the bot works.
  • Mod History: If someone gets warned, kicked, or banned, we keep a record of it (the "case log") to help mods keep track.
  • Messages (Sometimes): We do not store chat logs by default. We only save specific messages if you enable "Message Logging" or when a command is used.
  • Login Info: When you log into this dashboard, we use cookies and your IP to keep your session secure.
  • Bot Stats: We track basic things like "how many times was the !help command used" to optimize performance.
🎯 How We Use It
  • To Run the Bot: We use data to reply to commands, give you roles, and show you pretty graphs on this dashboard.
  • To Keep It Safe: We use data for AutoMod (like catching bad words) and to stop spammers.
  • To Fix Bugs: If the bot crashes, we look at error logs to see what went wrong.
  • To Talk to You: Sometimes the bot needs to DM you (like "You were warned in Server X") or post in a log channel.
  • To Follow the Law: We have to follow the rules, including Discord's Terms of Service.
🧯 Keeping It Safe
  • Encryption: We scramble sensitive stuff like tokens and passwords so nobody can read them.
  • Locked Doors: Only the main developers can access the database, and we use secure providers.
  • Less is More: We try not to hoard data. If we don't need it for a feature, we don't save it.
  • Cleaning House: The owner has options to delete certain rows of data.
