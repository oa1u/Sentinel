📌 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how this Discord bot and how collects, uses, stores, and protects data while providing moderation, automation, and community-management features.

The policy applies to server owners, administrators, moderators, and members whose data is processed through bot commands, moderation actions, ticket flows, and audit features.

By using this system, administrators acknowledge responsibility for configuring features in line with their community rules and applicable platform requirements.

📦 Data We Process
  • Platform identifiers: Discord user IDs, guild/server IDs, role IDs, and channel IDs used to map actions and permissions.
  • Moderation history: warnings, kicks, bans, unbans, timeouts, case IDs, reasons, moderator identifiers, and timestamps.
  • Audit logs: event metadata and, when enabled, message content snapshots (before and after) for edits/deletions.
  • Tickets and appeals: ticket status, participants, appeal statements, decisions, and contact email provided by the submitter.
  • Admin panel accounts: usernames, roles, email addresses, password hashes, encrypted 2FA secrets, recovery code hashes, trusted device metadata, and optional linked Discord IDs.
  • Authentication events: login/logout activity, IP address, user agent, session data, and security-related events.
  • Operational metrics: command usage, verification analytics, activity tracking, and automation outcomes for reliability and safety.
  • Configuration data: server-specific settings for automod, logging channels, verification, leveling, backups, and feature toggles.
  • Backups: database exports stored on the server when backups are enabled.
🎯 How Data Is Used
  • To execute requested bot features, including moderation workflows, tickets, utility commands, and community-management tools.
  • To maintain safety controls such as automod checks, suspicious-pattern detection, and repeat-offense tracking.
  • To provide accountability through case histories, moderation logs, and privileged action traceability.
  • To enforce role-based access in the so users only access tools permitted by their role.
  • To improve stability and feature quality through diagnostics and aggregate performance monitoring.
  • To comply with lawful requests and platform safety obligations when required.
🧯 Security & Retention
  • Access control: administrative features are restricted by authentication, role checks, and protected sessions.
  • Auditability: sensitive actions are logged to support incident investigation and moderation transparency.
  • Data minimization: only data necessary for configured features and moderation operations is processed.
  • Retention practice: records are retained until deleted by administrators or rotated by configured backup retention settings. There is no automatic global expiration unless configured.
  • Safeguards: credentials are stored as hashes, and 2FA secrets are stored encrypted. Additional controls reduce unauthorized access and misuse.
  • Third-party platform dependency: some data handling occurs through Discord infrastructure and is subject to Discord platform behavior.
  • Email delivery: if SMTP is enabled, email delivery logs may include recipient address, template name, and delivery status.
🗂️ Your Rights

Depending on your role and local requirements, you may request actions related to data processed by this system.

  • Review moderation or ticket records related to your account where policy permits.
  • Request correction of inaccurate records or context notes.
  • Request deletion of records that are no longer necessary, subject to operational and legal constraints.
  • Request explanation of automated moderation outcomes that affected your account.

Requests are handled by server management or designated administrators, and may be limited where retention is required for security, dispute resolution, or compliance.
