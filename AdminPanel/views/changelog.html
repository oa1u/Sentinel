Major Update 7.0.0

Reliability, security, and operator-focused upgrades across the bot and the panel.

March 2026
  • FeatureMusic playback is more reliable, with smarter retries and a richer now playing view.
  • FeatureLyrics lookups are now built into the music commands.
  • FeatureRisk-based verification now ties into anti-raid signals for suspicious joins.

  • FeatureAnti-raid flows integrate directly with verification checks.
  • FeatureChannel revival nudges inactive chats back to life.
  • FeatureAdmin Panel now supports a 2FA login challenge with clearer security prompts.
  • FeatureWebsite-to-bot webhooks now route events with better audit visibility.
  • EnhancementJoin-to-Create rooms auto-clean when idle.
  • EnhancementDiscord account linking is now required before browsing other panel pages.
  • EnhancementFAQ and Features pages were refreshed to reflect newer systems.
